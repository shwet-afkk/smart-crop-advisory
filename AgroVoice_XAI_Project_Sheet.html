🔬 Box 1: Visual Explainable AI (Grad-CAM) Active XAI

Neural Saliency Grounding: Intercepts forward activations & backward gradients at features[-1] to highlight fungal necrotic lesions with zero human bias.

🔍 How the AI Sees the Leaf (In Plain Words):
• Red & Yellow Hotspots: Highlights active fungal spots triggering diagnosis.
• Cool Blue Regions: Indicates healthy green areas safely ignored.
• Visual Proof (XAI): Gives farmers 100% confidence diagnosis is real.

💊 Box 4: 4-Pillar Agronomic Knowledge Engine KVK Verified
1. Etiology / Cause: Pathogen identification in simple words (e.g., fungal spore infection via high humidity).
2. Organic Remedies: Cow Urine (Gomutra), Neem Oil 5ml/L, Buttermilk, and Trichoderma viride.
3. Chemical Spray & Dosage: Saaf / Ridomil Gold @ 2g/L (30g per 15-Liter backpack spray pump) + PHI window.
4. Cultural Prevention: Crop rotation, soil aeration, drip irrigation, and plucking infected leaves.

🖨️ Box 6: Report Card & WhatsApp Integration 1-Click Share

Closing the Rural Delivery Gap: Generates instant, verifiable digital and physical prescription cards.

• 📥 Official PDF Health Prescription: Ready-to-print single page card with GPS, leaf photo, Grad-CAM heatmap, and exact pump dosages.
• 📲 1-Tap WhatsApp Share: Pre-formatted message sent directly to local pesticide dealers or agricultural extension officers (KVK).
• 🔒 Domain Shift Safeguard: Out-of-Distribution thresholding preventing false prescriptions on non-leaf images.
