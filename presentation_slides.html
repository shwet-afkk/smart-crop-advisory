I. Problem Statement
🌾 1. Severe Yield Loss & Delayed Diagnosis: Crop diseases destroy 20–40% of yields annually. Farmers lack on-field access to agricultural extension scientists during critical infection windows.
📦 2. Black-Box AI Trust Deficit: Existing deep learning models output opaque labels without visual localization, causing farmer skepticism and misdiagnosis.
🌦️ 3. Meteorological Disconnection: Models diagnose leaves in isolation without factoring in ambient humidity (≥75%) and rainfall wetness that drive fungal multiplication.
🗣️ 4. Language & Practicality Barrier: Text-heavy English outputs fail illiterate farmers and omit practical 15-liter backpack spray pump dosages.

II. Objectives
🎯 1. High-Accuracy Edge Deep Learning: Deploy MobileNetV2 classifying 39 crop taxa at 98.43% accuracy with <30 ms latency.
🔬 2. Visual Explainability (Grad-CAM): Generate gradient heatmaps visually highlighting active fungal necrotic lesions.
🌦️ 3. Real-Time Microclimate Risk Engine: Ingest Open-Meteo GPS weather to compute Disease Triangle risk score (0–100).
🗣️ 4. Multilingual Zero-Literacy Voice Assistant: Build interactive Web Speech voice assistant in Kannada, Hindi, and English.
🖨️ 5. Instant Rural Deliverables: 1-Click printable PDF health prescription card and 1-tap WhatsApp sharing for local dealers.

IV. Analysis: Existing System vs. Proposed System
EXISTING SYSTEM

• Manual Field Scouting: Slow, labor-intensive, error-prone, and dependent on scarce agricultural scientists.

• Black-Box AI Models: Output opaque prediction labels with zero visual confirmation of which leaf lesions triggered the diagnosis.

• Isolated Vision (No Weather): Diagnoses image without checking today's humidity (82%) or rain, failing to forecast disease multiplication.

• Text-Heavy English Jargon: Inaccessible to illiterate or vernacular-speaking rural farmers.

• Vague Chemical Advice: Mentions chemical names without practical 15-liter backpack pump spray dosages.

PROPOSED: AGROVOICE-XAI

• Lightweight Edge MobileNetV2: 98.43% validation accuracy, 2.2M parameters, and 22 ms inference latency across 39 crop classes.

• Active Grad-CAM Saliency (XAI): Generates visual glowing red/yellow heatmaps over active fungal necrotic lesions for 100% farmer trust.

• Live Weather Triangulation: Open-Meteo GPS weather telemetry computes live Disease Triangle spread risk score (0–100).

• Zero-Literacy Vernacular Voice: Interactive hands-free speech recognition (STT) and voice synthesis (TTS) in Kannada, Hindi, and English.

• 4-Pillar Calibrated Prescriptions: Organic remedies + exact 30g/15L pump chemical spray dosages + 1-click WhatsApp and PDF share.

VI. System Architecture & Data Flow Design
1. Client Edge Layer (React 18 + Vite): Camera viewfinder, aspect ratio alignment, Web Speech API (STT/TTS).
2. FastAPI Gateway: Asynchronous payload decoding, tensor normalization (224x224x3 ImageNet).
3. Parallel Deep Learning & Weather: MobileNetV2 (98.43%) + Grad-CAM hook at features[-1] + Open-Meteo Disease Triangle risk engine.
4. 4-Pillar Agronomic Knowledge Engine: Etiology, Organic remedies, 30g/15L pump chemical dosage, Cultural prevention.
5. Deliverables: Side-by-side XAI view, Kannada/Hindi audio playback, 1-click PDF card & WhatsApp share.
